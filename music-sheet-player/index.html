Tips for a good read

  • Reads printed staves: treble or bass clef, key and time signatures, whole to sixteenth notes, dots, accidentals, rests and simple chords. Piano music (treble and bass staves joined by a brace) plays both hands together.
  • Shoot straight at the page with the staves level and filling the frame, in even light without shadows.
  • Not yet supported in the browser: handwritten music, two melodies on one staff, ties and triplets. With the AI reader on your own computer, ties, triplets and two voices are read too.
